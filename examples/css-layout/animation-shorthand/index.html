One line, or eight

animation: bounce 1s ease-in-out infinite alternate
the same thing, written longhand

Both balls move identically. The first uses the shorthand; the second sets animation-name, animation-duration, animation-timing-function, animation-iteration-count and animation-direction separately.

⚠ The shorthand resets what you leave out

animation: bounce 1s
animation-name: bounce; animation-duration: 1s;

Identical here — but the shorthand also resets animation-delay, iteration-count, direction and fill-mode to their initial values. That is why a shorthand written after a longhand throws the longhand away.
