colour + image

The colour is painted first, the image sits in front of it. Where the image is transparent, the colour shows through.

the paint stops at the border

a 14px dashed border, over a background

The background is painted out to the outside of the border — you can see it through the gaps in the dashes. It never crosses into the margin, which is always transparent.

That last one is the whole box model in one box: the background covers the content, the padding and the border area, the border is drawn on top of it, and the margin outside stays clear.
