a flat colour

background-color: #185fa5

One colour behind everything in the box.

an image painted on top of the colour

colour + image

The colour goes down first, the image over it. That PNG is transparent around the tree, so the blue keeps showing through — wherever the file is opaque, the colour is hidden.

the paint stops at the border

A 14px dashed border over a tiled image. The background is painted all the way out to the outside of the border — look at the gaps between the dashes. It never crosses into the margin, which is always transparent.

That last one is the whole box model in one box: the background covers the content, the padding and the border area, the border is drawn on top of it, and the margin outside stays clear.
